About This System

This quiz was created to help first-year students and beginners explore the computer science field without requiring previous coding experience. It compares your interests and preferences across 16 specializations, then suggests the branch personality that fits you best along with three close matches.

Use the result as a starting point for discovering subjects, projects, and careers you may enjoy—not as a permanent label or final career decision.
